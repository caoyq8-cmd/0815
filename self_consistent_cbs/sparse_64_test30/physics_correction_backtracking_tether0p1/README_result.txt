Self-consistent CBS adjoint physics correction on sparse-64 test30.

Dataset:
- Processed OpenBreastUS oldstyle dataset.
- target_256 is bilinearly upsampled to target_480.
- self-consistent dobs are generated by current cbs_model.py.
- Sparse measurement: 64 sources / 64 receivers.
- Frequency: 500 kHz.

Correction:
- Initial reconstruction: InversionNet condition.
- Coordinate conversion: x_phys = x_img.T.
- Correction steps: 8.
- Backtracking: enabled.
- prior_tether = 0.1.
- step_size_mps = 1.0.
- smooth_kernel = 9.

Aggregate over 30 test samples:
- image_mse_256: 160.3046 -> 110.0548.
- image_mse_480: 149.5388 -> 101.6461.
- dobs_abs_loss: 0.009363 -> 0.004938.

Improvement:
- image_mse_256 reduced by 32.84% on average.
- image_mse_480 reduced by 33.89% on average.
- dobs_abs_loss reduced by 47.48% on average.
- 30/30 samples improved in image_mse_256.
- 30/30 samples improved in image_mse_480.
- 30/30 samples improved in measurement loss.

Conclusion:
Self-consistent CBS adjoint physics correction consistently improves both image-domain reconstruction accuracy and measurement consistency. This validates the physics-guided refinement route and motivates replacing slow CBS adjoint computation with a neural operator surrogate.
