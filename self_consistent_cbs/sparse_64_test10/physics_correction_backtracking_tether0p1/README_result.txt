Self-consistent CBS adjoint physics correction on sparse-64 test10.

Dataset:
- Processed OpenBreastUS oldstyle dataset
- target_256 upsampled to target_480 by bilinear interpolation
- self-consistent dobs generated by current cbs_model.py
- sparse measurement: 64 sources / 64 receivers
- frequency: 500 kHz

Correction:
- initial reconstruction: InversionNet condition
- coordinate conversion: x_phys = x_img.T
- correction steps: 8
- backtracking: enabled
- prior_tether = 0.1
- step_size_mps = 1.0
- smooth_kernel = 9

Aggregate over 10 test samples:
- image_mse_256: 115.9493 -> 80.9609
- image_mse_480: 107.3605 -> 73.7263
- dobs_abs_loss: 0.008409 -> 0.004116

Improvement:
- image_mse_256 reduced by 32.06% on average
- image_mse_480 reduced by 33.99% on average
- dobs_abs_loss reduced by 51.72% on average
- 10/10 samples improved in image_mse_256
- 10/10 samples improved in image_mse_480
- 10/10 samples improved in measurement loss

Conclusion:
CBS adjoint physics correction consistently improves both image-domain reconstruction accuracy and measurement consistency. This validates the physics-guided refinement route and motivates replacing slow CBS adjoint computation with a neural operator surrogate.
